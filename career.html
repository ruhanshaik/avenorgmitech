What We Believe
Our operating principles
We don't have a playbook — we write it. These are the values that guide every decision we make.

Signal over noise

We optimize for clarity, insight, and signal in everything we build. If it adds noise, it doesn't ship.

Ship weekly

We deploy code every week. Small, fast iterations beat perfect, delayed launches.

Geography is not destiny

The best talent isn't just in SF or Bangalore. We build remote-first and hire from anywhere.

Radical transparency

We share metrics, mistakes, and learnings openly. No ego, no hidden agendas.

Founder-first

Everything we do serves the founder. If it doesn't help them, we shouldn't build it.

Long-term greed

We optimize for the next decade, not the next quarter. Sustainable over explosive.
