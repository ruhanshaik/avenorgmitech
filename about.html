The Founder
"Capital should flow to signal, not to whoever has the loudest network. AVENOR is how we get there."

PRANAV GAUTAM

Founder & CEO · Building the future of venture intelligence

PRANAV GAUTAM started building internet products at 14 from Bihar, one of India's most underestimated states. By 2024, he had watched dozens of talented founders waste months on broken fundraising rituals — cold outreach chaos, static pitch decks, unstructured investor meetings. He knew there had to be a better way. AVENOR is his answer: a full-stack venture intelligence platform that replaces spreadsheets and scattered Notion links with structured, AI-powered decision infrastructure.

The Problem
Startup investing is broken by noise.
The fundraising stack hasn't changed in a decade. AVENOR rebuilds it from the ground up — one structured workflow at a time.
01

Cold Outreach Chaos

Founders blast thousands of investors. Investors drown in unfiltered intros and warm-loops. Neither side wins.

02

Pitch Deck Spam

Static PDFs over email — no context, no continuity, no way to track what changed between meetings.

03

Unstructured Evaluation

Every fund re-invents the wheel: spreadsheets, Notion, scattered Loom links. No intelligence layer underneath.

04

Static Fundraising

A round becomes a six-month sprint of one-off updates instead of a living conversation with real-time data.

05

Discovery Inefficiency

High-signal startups are invisible. Low-signal noise floats to the top because the loudest wins — not the best.

06

No Accountability Layer

Founders disappear post-cheque. Investors lose visibility on the bets they made. Trust erodes slowly.

Our Values
What we believe in.
Every product decision at AVENOR flows from a small set of deeply held beliefs about how capital should work — and who it should serve.

Signal Over Noise

Every feature we ship asks one question: does this increase the signal-to-noise ratio for both founders and investors? If not, it doesn't ship.

Trust as Infrastructure

Verification isn't a feature — it's the foundation. Every startup and investor on AVENOR is verified across multiple dimensions continuously.

Geography is Not Destiny

The next great startup can come from Bihar, Kano, or Bogotá. We build tools that give every founder an equal shot at being discovered.

Speed with Structure

Fundraising should move fast — but with structure, not chaos. We compress timelines without sacrificing the depth that good decisions require.

Radical Transparency

No hidden success fees. No carry on your round. No algorithmic black boxes. We show our work — because opacity is the enemy of trust.

Founder First

Investors are important — but founders build the future. Every product decision is weighed first against how it serves the person doing the building.

The Vision

A world where signal wins.

A world where every meaningful startup has a structured way to be evaluated — and every serious investor has a calm, high-signal way to find them. Where geography isn't a barrier and pedigree doesn't substitute for proof. Less noise. Better decisions. Built from Bihar, for the world.
